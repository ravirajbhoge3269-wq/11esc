Welcome to London

11 East Street Cafe born in 2007 is an iconic establishment in Pune. The double decker bus is a signature attraction of the outlet. 11ESC as it is lovingly called is known to serve a fantastic variety of food. Their bar program is very strong and one has to come over and appreciate the service and food they provide at their all day eatery and bar. 11 East Street Cafe serving you an amazing butter chicken as well as a superb negroni. From indian to oriental to international food, they have it all. Go ahead, call, make a reservation and see what they deliver! IN SERVICE since 2007
